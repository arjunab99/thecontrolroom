FLOW ID: RE-LQ-001 SALES & LEAD MANAGEMENT

Lead Qualification & Smart Assignment System

Your 70% leads are waste. We qualify leads before they reach your sales team — saving your salespersons' time and your money. Automated WhatsApp survey asks 3‑5 qualifying questions (budget, timeline, location, property type). Hot leads (score ≥70) get instant alert to the best‑performing salesperson. Cold leads get auto‑nurtured — no sales time wasted. Up to 40% reduction in sales headcount needed because your team only talks to real buyers.

EXECUTION SEQUENCE

[INCOMING LEAD CAPTURED FROM ANY CHANNEL] → [WHATSAPP SURVEY SENT AUTOMATICALLY] → [ANSWERS PARSED & SCORED] → [HOT LEADS ASSIGNED TO TOP SALESPERSON] → [COLD LEADS ADDED TO AUTOMATED NURTURE SEQUENCE] → [SALESPERSON SAVES 15+ HOURS PER WEEK] → [YOU REDUCE SALES TEAM SIZE WITHOUT LOSING REVENUE]

FLOW ID: RE-TM-002 CUSTOMER SUPPORT

Ticket Master – Post‑Sales Complaint Lifecycle

Every complaint tracked from open to close. No ticket lost. No customer ignored. No RERA escalation. Every WhatsApp, email, or call becomes a ticket with a full lifecycle (Open → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved → Closed). Auto‑escalation if not resolved in 48 hours — sends alert to manager. Unresolved after 72 hours goes to regional head. Reduces customer support staff by 50% because automated workflows handle follow‑ups, reminders, and status updates.

EXECUTION SEQUENCE

[COMPLAINT RECEIVED VIA WHATSAPP/EMAIL/CALL] → [TICKET CREATED WITH UNIQUE ID] → [AUTO‑CATEGORISED & PRIORITISED] → [ASSIGNED TO RIGHT AGENT] → [CUSTOMER RECEIVES AUTO‑ACKNOWLEDGMENT] → [DAILY STATUS UPDATES SENT AUTOMATICALLY] → [ESCALATED IF UNRESOLVED IN 48h] → [RESOLVED & CLOSED WITH CUSTOMER CONFIRMATION] → [MANAGEMENT DASHBOARD SHOWS REAL‑TIME TICKET AGING]

EXECUTION SEQUENCE
